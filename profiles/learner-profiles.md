---
title: 'Learner Profiles'
---

This lesson targets people who are new to the EIC software environment — students, postdocs, and
researchers joining the ePIC collaboration who need a working `eic-shell` and a GitHub account
before starting the other [EIC tutorials](https://eic.github.io/documentation/tutorials.html). No
prior experience with containers or the EIC software stack is assumed.
